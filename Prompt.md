Phase 1 – Core Team & Task Tracking (MVP)
User Management
* Create users (engineers, managers, admins)
* Roles: Admin, Manager, Engineer, Viewer

 Issue / Task Tracking
* Create issues/tickets
* Assign to users
* Status workflow:
    * Open → In Progress → Blocked → Resolved → Closed

Phase 2 – SLA & Performance Tracking (Your Unique Value)
SLA Configuration
* Define SLA per project or ticket type:
    * P1: 15 min response, 1 hr resolution
    * P2: 1 hr response, 4 hr resolution
    * P3: 24 hr resolution
SLA Monitoring
* Track:
    * Response time
    * Resolution time
    * SLA breached or met
* Show dashboards per engineer/team
KPIs
* Tickets resolved per engineer
* MTTR (Mean Time to Resolve)
* SLA compliance %


Phase 3 – Advanced Enterprise Features
 Notifications
* Email, Teams alerts for SLA breach

Audit & Compliance
* Change logs (who changed what)
* Export reports (PCI, ISO, SOC compliance)
